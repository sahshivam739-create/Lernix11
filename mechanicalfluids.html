💧 Mechanical Properties of Fluids

Class 11 Physics • Lernix11

1. Pressure

💧 ⬇️ 🧱

📖 Definition

Pressure is the normal force acting per unit area of a surface.

P = F/A
⭐ SI Unit:

Pascal (Pa)

2. Pressure in a Fluid

💧⬇️💧⬇️

📖 Concept

The pressure in a fluid at rest increases with depth because of the weight of the fluid above it.

P = P₀ + ρgh

3. Pascal's Law

💧 🔄 💪

📖 Statement

Pressure applied to an enclosed fluid is transmitted equally and undiminished in all directions throughout the fluid.

⭐ Applications:

Hydraulic lift, hydraulic brakes and hydraulic press.

4. Buoyancy

🪵 💧 ⬆️

📖 Definition

Buoyancy is the upward force exerted by a fluid on an object partially or completely immersed in it.

⭐ Important:

The buoyant force acts in the upward direction.

5. Archimedes' Principle

🪨 💧 ⬆️

📖 Statement

A body immersed partly or completely in a fluid experiences an upward buoyant force equal to the weight of the fluid displaced by it.

Buoyant Force = Weight of displaced fluid

6. Equation of Continuity

💧 ➡️ 🔵 ➡️ 🔴

📖 Concept

For a steady flow of an incompressible fluid, the mass flowing per second remains constant.

A₁v₁ = A₂v₂
⭐ Important:

Where A is cross-sectional area and v is the speed of fluid.

7. Bernoulli's Principle

💨 💧 ➡️ 🚀

📖 Statement

For a steady, incompressible and non-viscous fluid flow, the total energy per unit volume remains constant along a streamline.

P + 1/2ρv² + ρgh = Constant
⭐ Applications:

Venturimeter, atomizer, lift on an airplane wing and flow through a narrow pipe.

8. Viscosity

💧 🐢 ➡️ 💧 ⚡

📖 Definition

Viscosity is the internal resistance offered by a fluid to the relative motion between its layers.

⭐ Example:

Honey has greater viscosity than water, so it flows more slowly.

9. Stokes' Law

⚫ 💧 ⬇️

📖 Statement

The viscous force on a small spherical body moving slowly through a fluid is proportional to the radius of the sphere and its speed.

F = 6πηrv

10. Terminal Velocity

⚫ ⬇️ 💧

📖 Definition

Terminal velocity is the constant maximum velocity attained by a body falling through a viscous fluid when the net force on it becomes zero.

vₜ = 2r²(ρ - σ)g / 9η

11. Surface Tension

💧 🫧 ✨

📖 Definition

Surface tension is the property of a liquid surface by virtue of which it behaves like a stretched elastic membrane.

⭐ Examples:

Small drops of liquid tend to be spherical and some insects can move on the surface of water.

12. Excess Pressure

🫧 💧 ⭕

📖 Concept

The pressure inside a curved liquid surface is greater than the pressure outside it.

Liquid drop: ΔP = 2T/R

Soap bubble: ΔP = 4T/R

13. Capillarity

💧 ⬆️ 🧪

📖 Definition

Capillarity is the rise or fall of a liquid in a narrow tube due to surface tension.

⭐ Examples:

Rise of water in plants and absorption of water by blotting paper.
