1. Introduction to Conic Sections

📖 Concept

A conic section is a curve obtained by the intersection of a plane with a double cone.

The main conic sections are:

• Circle

• Parabola

• Ellipse

• Hyperbola

✏️ Ex 1

Name the conic represented by:

x² + y² = 25

✅ Solution

The equation is of the form:

x² + y² = r²

Therefore it represents a circle.

Answer: Circle

✏️ Ex 2

Identify the conic:

y² = 4x

✅ Solution

The standard form of a parabola is:

y² = 4ax

Therefore, it is a parabola.

⭕ 🎯 📏

2. Circle

A circle is the locus of a point which moves in a plane such that its distance from a fixed point remains constant.

The fixed point is called the centre and the constant distance is called the radius.

Standard equation: (x−h)² + (y−k)² = r²

Centre = (h,k)
Radius = r

✏️ Ex 1

Find the centre and radius of:

(x−2)² + (y−3)² = 16

✅ Solution

Compare with:

(x−h)² + (y−k)² = r²

h = 2, k = 3

r² = 16

Centre = (2,3), Radius = 4

✏️ Ex 2

Find the equation of a circle with centre (1,−2) and radius 5.

✅ Solution

Using:

(x−h)² + (y−k)² = r²

(x−1)² + (y+2)² = 25

Required equation: (x−1)² + (y+2)² = 25

🔵 ➡️ 📈

3. Parabola

A parabola is the locus of a point which moves so that its distance from a fixed point is equal to its distance from a fixed line.

The fixed point is called the focus and the fixed line is called the directrix.

Standard forms:

y² = 4ax
y² = −4ax
x² = 4ay
x² = −4ay

✏️ Ex 1

Find the focus of:

y² = 12x

✅ Solution

Compare:

y² = 4ax

4a = 12

a = 3

Focus = (a,0)

Focus = (3,0)

✏️ Ex 2

Find the focus of:

x² = 8y

✅ Solution

Compare:

x² = 4ay

4a = 8

a = 2

Focus = (0,2)

⬭ 📐 🔍

4. Ellipse

An ellipse is the locus of a point such that the sum of its distances from two fixed points is constant.

The two fixed points are called foci.

Standard equation: x²/a² + y²/b² = 1

For a > b: c² = a² − b²
Foci = (±c,0)

✏️ Ex 1

Find the vertices of:

x²/25 + y²/9 = 1

✅ Solution

a² = 25

a = 5

Since the major axis is along x-axis:

Vertices = (±5,0)

✏️ Ex 2

Find c for:

x²/25 + y²/16 = 1

✅ Solution

a² = 25

b² = 16

c² = a² − b²

= 25 − 16

= 9

c = 3

↔️ 🔵 📐

5. Hyperbola

A hyperbola is the locus of a point such that the difference of its distances from two fixed points is constant.

The two fixed points are called foci.

Standard equation: x²/a² − y²/b² = 1

c² = a² + b²
Foci = (±c,0)

✏️ Ex 1

Find c for:

x²/9 − y²/16 = 1

✅ Solution

a² = 9

b² = 16

c² = a² + b²

= 9 + 16

= 25

c = 5

✏️ Ex 2 — 🔴 Hard

Find the vertices of:

x²/36 − y²/25 = 1

✅ Solution

a² = 36

a = 6

The transverse axis is along x-axis.

Vertices = (±6,0)

📊 📐 ⭐

6. Eccentricity

Eccentricity is a constant associated with a conic section. It helps distinguish different conics.

Circle: e = 0

Ellipse: 0 < e < 1

Parabola: e = 1

Hyperbola: e > 1

✏️ Ex 1

What is the eccentricity of a parabola?

✅ Solution

For a parabola:

e = 1

✏️ Ex 2

What is the range of eccentricity of an ellipse?

✅ Solution

For an ellipse:

0 < e < 1

⭐ Important Formula Revision

1. Circle: (x−h)²+(y−k)²=r²

2. Parabola: y²=4ax

3. Parabola: x²=4ay

4. Ellipse: x²/a²+y²/b²=1

5. Ellipse: c²=a²−b²

6. Hyperbola: x²/a²−y²/b²=1

7. Hyperbola: c²=a²+b²

8. Circle eccentricity = 0

9. Parabola eccentricity = 1

10. Ellipse: 0<e<1

11. Hyperbola: e>1
