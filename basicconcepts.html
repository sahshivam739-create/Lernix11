🧪 Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry

Class 11 Chemistry • Lernix11

1. Matter

⚛️ 🧪 💧

📖 Definition

Matter is anything that has mass and occupies space.

⭐ Classification:

Matter can be classified as elements, compounds and mixtures.

2. Laws of Chemical Combination

⚗️ ⚖️ 🧪

📖 Important Laws

The important laws are:

1. Law of Conservation of Mass

2. Law of Definite Proportions

3. Law of Multiple Proportions

3. Atomic Mass

⚛️ ⚖️

📖 Definition

Atomic mass is the relative mass of an atom compared with 1/12 of the mass of one carbon-12 atom.

⭐ Unit:

u (unified atomic mass unit)

4. Molecular Mass

⚛️ ➕ ⚛️

📖 Definition

Molecular mass is the sum of the atomic masses of all atoms present in one molecule.

5. Mole Concept

🔢 ⚛️ ⚛️ ⚛️

📖 Definition

A mole is the amount of substance containing exactly 6.022 × 10²³ elementary entities.

1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ particles
⭐ Avogadro Constant:

NA = 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹

6. Molar Mass

⚖️ 🧪 🔢

📖 Definition

Molar mass is the mass of one mole of a substance.

Molar Mass = Mass / Number of moles
⭐ Unit:

g mol⁻¹

7. Percentage Composition

📊 🧪 ⚛️

📖 Definition

Percentage composition gives the percentage by mass of each element present in a compound.

% of element = (Mass of element in one mole of compound / Molar mass of compound) × 100

8. Empirical Formula

🧪 ➗ ⚛️

📖 Definition

The empirical formula represents the simplest whole-number ratio of atoms of different elements in a compound.

⭐ Example:

For glucose, the empirical formula is CH₂O.

9. Molecular Formula

⚛️ ➕ ⚛️ 🧪

📖 Definition

The molecular formula shows the actual number of atoms of each element present in one molecule of a compound.

Molecular Formula = n × Empirical Formula

10. Stoichiometry

⚖️ ➡️ 🧪 ➡️ ⚗️

📖 Definition

Stoichiometry deals with the quantitative relationships between reactants and products in a chemical reaction.

⭐ Important:

A balanced chemical equation is essential for stoichiometric calculations.

11. Limiting Reagent

⚗️ ⛔ 🧪

📖 Definition

The reactant that is completely consumed first and limits the amount of product formed is called the limiting reagent.

🔴 JEE Important:

Always compare the available amount of each reactant with its stoichiometric requirement.

12. Concentration of Solutions

🧪 💧 🔢

📖 Important Terms

Concentration tells us the amount of solute present in a given amount of solution or solvent.

Molarity = Moles of solute / Volume of solution in litre

Molality = Moles of solute / Mass of solvent in kg

13. Mole Fraction

⚛️ ➗ ⚛️

📖 Definition

Mole fraction of a component is the ratio of its number of moles to the total number of moles present in the solution.

Xₐ = nₐ / (nₐ + nᵦ)

⭐ Important Formula Revision

🧠 🧪 🔢
n = Given Mass / Molar Mass

N = n × NA

Molarity = Moles / Volume in litre

Molality = Moles / Mass of solvent in kg

% Composition = (Mass of element / Molar mass) × 100
