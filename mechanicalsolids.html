🧱 Mechanical Properties of Solids

Class 11 Physics • Lernix11

1. Elasticity

🧱 ↔️ 🧲

📖 Definition

Elasticity is the property of a material by virtue of which it regains its original shape and size when the deforming force is removed.

2. Stress

💪 ➡️ 🧱

📖 Definition

Stress is the restoring force developed per unit area of a body when it is deformed.

Stress = Force / Area

3. Strain

🧱 ↔️ 📏

📖 Definition

Strain is the ratio of change in dimension to the original dimension.

Strain = Change in dimension / Original dimension

4. Hooke's Law

🪀 ↔️ 💪

📖 Statement

Within the elastic limit, stress is directly proportional to strain.

Stress ∝ Strain

5. Young's Modulus

🧱 📏 ↔️

📖 Definition

Young's modulus is the ratio of longitudinal stress to longitudinal strain within the elastic limit.

Y = Longitudinal Stress / Longitudinal Strain

6. Bulk Modulus

🌊 🧊 💪

📖 Definition

Bulk modulus is the ratio of hydraulic stress to volumetric strain.

K = - ΔP / (ΔV/V)

7. Shear Modulus

🧱 ↗️ ↘️

📖 Definition

Shear modulus is the ratio of tangential stress to shear strain within the elastic limit.

G = Shear Stress / Shear Strain

8. Stress-Strain Curve

📈 🧱

📖 Concept

A stress-strain curve shows how a material deforms when stress is applied to it.

⭐ Important Points:

Elastic region, yield point, plastic region and breaking point are important parts of the curve.

9. Elastic Potential Energy

🪀 ⚡

📖 Concept

Energy stored in a body due to elastic deformation is called elastic potential energy.

Energy = 1/2 × Stress × Strain × Volume

10. Applications of Elasticity

🏗️ 🌉 🧱

📖 Important

Elasticity is important in designing buildings, bridges, cranes, wires and other structures.

⭐ Remember:

Materials used in structures must be able to withstand the required stress without permanent deformation or failure.

⭐ Quick Revision
