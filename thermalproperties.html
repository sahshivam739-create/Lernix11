🌡️ Thermal Properties of Matter

Class 11 Physics • Lernix11

1. Heat and Temperature

🌡️ 🔥

📖 Temperature

Temperature is a physical quantity that indicates the degree of hotness or coldness of a body.

⭐ Heat:

Heat is energy transferred between bodies due to a temperature difference.

2. Thermal Expansion

🔥 ➡️ 📏

📖 Definition

Thermal expansion is the increase in dimensions of a substance when its temperature is increased.

⭐ Types:

Linear expansion, area expansion and volume expansion.

3. Linear Expansion

📏 🔥 ↔️

📖 Concept

The increase in length of a solid due to increase in temperature is called linear expansion.

ΔL = αL₀ΔT

4. Area Expansion

⬛ 🔥 ↔️

📖 Concept

The increase in area of a solid due to an increase in temperature is called area expansion.

ΔA = βA₀ΔT

5. Volume Expansion

🧊 🔥 📦

📖 Concept

The increase in volume of a substance due to an increase in temperature is called volume expansion.

ΔV = γV₀ΔT

6. Specific Heat Capacity

🔥 💧 🌡️

📖 Definition

Specific heat capacity is the amount of heat required to raise the temperature of unit mass of a substance by 1°C or 1 K.

Q = mcΔT
⭐ SI Unit:

J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹

7. Calorimetry

🔥 ⚖️ 💧

📖 Principle

In an isolated system, heat lost by the hotter body is equal to heat gained by the colder body.

Heat lost = Heat gained

8. Change of State

🧊 ➡️ 💧 ➡️ 💨

📖 Concept

A substance can change from solid to liquid, liquid to gas and vice versa by changing temperature or pressure.

⭐ Important:

During a change of state, temperature remains constant while heat is absorbed or released.

9. Latent Heat

🧊 🔥 💧

📖 Definition

Latent heat is the heat required to change the state of unit mass of a substance without changing its temperature.

Q = mL

10. Heat Transfer

🔥 ➡️ 🌡️

📖 Types

Heat can be transferred by conduction, convection and radiation.

⭐ Three Modes:

1. Conduction
2. Convection
3. Radiation

11. Conduction

🔥 🪙 ➡️ 🪙

📖 Definition

Conduction is the transfer of heat through a substance without bulk movement of the substance.

⭐ Example:

Heat travelling through a metal rod when one end is heated.

12. Convection

💧 🔄 🔥

📖 Definition

Convection is the transfer of heat by the actual movement of fluid particles.

⭐ Example:

Heating of water and formation of sea breeze.

13. Radiation

☀️ ➡️ 🌍

📖 Definition

Radiation is the transfer of heat in the form of electromagnetic waves and does not require a material medium.

⭐ Example:

Heat from the Sun reaches Earth by radiation.

14. Newton's Law of Cooling

☕ ➡️ 🌡️

📖 Statement

The rate of loss of heat of a body is approximately proportional to the difference between its temperature and the temperature of its surroundings, for small temperature differences.
