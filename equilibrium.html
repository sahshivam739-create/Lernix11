⚖️ Equilibrium

Class 11 Chemistry • Lernix11

1. Chemical Equilibrium

⚗️ ⇌ ⚗️

📖 Definition

Chemical equilibrium is the state of a reversible reaction in which the rates of forward and backward reactions become equal.

⭐ Important:

At equilibrium, concentrations of reactants and products remain constant, but the reaction continues at the molecular level.

2. Reversible Reaction

🧪 ⇌ 🧪

📖 Definition

A reaction that can proceed in both forward and backward directions under suitable conditions is called a reversible reaction.

3. Dynamic Equilibrium

⚖️ 🔄 ⚖️

📖 Concept

Chemical equilibrium is dynamic because both forward and backward reactions continue even though their rates are equal.

4. Law of Mass Action

⚛️ ➕ ⚛️ ➡️ 🧪

📖 Statement

At a given temperature, the rate of a chemical reaction is proportional to the product of the active masses of the reactants.

5. Equilibrium Constant Kc

⚖️ 🔢 🧪

📖 Formula

For a general reaction:

aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD

Kc = [C]ᶜ[D]ᵈ / [A]ᵃ[B]ᵇ
⭐ Important:

The value of Kc depends on temperature.

6. Equilibrium Constant Kp

💨 ⚖️ 🔢

📖 Concept

For gaseous reactions, equilibrium can also be expressed in terms of partial pressures.

Kp = Kc(RT)ᴰⁿ
⭐ Where:

Δn = moles of gaseous products − moles of gaseous reactants.

7. Le Chatelier's Principle

⚖️ 🔄 🌡️

📖 Statement

If a change is imposed on a system at equilibrium, the system shifts in the direction that tends to oppose the change.

⭐ Changes include:

Concentration, pressure and temperature.

8. Effect of Concentration

🧪 ➕ ⚖️

📖 Concept

Increasing the concentration of a reactant generally shifts the equilibrium towards the products.

9. Effect of Pressure

💨 ⬆️ ⚖️

📖 Concept

For gaseous equilibria, increasing pressure shifts equilibrium towards the side having fewer moles of gas.

⭐ Important:

Pressure has no effect when the number of gaseous moles is the same on both sides.

10. Effect of Temperature

🔥 🌡️ ⚖️

📖 Concept

Increasing temperature favours the direction of the reaction that absorbs heat.

⭐ Remember:

Temperature can change the value of the equilibrium constant.

11. Ionic Equilibrium

💧 ➕ ➖ ⚖️

📖 Concept

Ionic equilibrium deals with the equilibrium between ions and uncharged molecules in aqueous solutions.

12. Acids and Bases

🧪 H⁺ ⇌ OH⁻

📖 Concept

According to the Arrhenius concept, acids produce H⁺ ions in aqueous solution and bases produce OH⁻ ions.

13. pH Scale

🧪 📊 🔢

📖 Definition

pH is a measure of the hydrogen ion concentration of a solution.

pH = −log[H⁺]

pOH = −log[OH⁻]

14. Ionic Product of Water

💧 ⚛️ ➕ ➖

📖 Formula

Water undergoes slight ionisation to produce H⁺ and OH⁻ ions.

Kᴡ = [H⁺][OH⁻]
⭐ At 25°C:

Kᴡ = 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴

15. Buffer Solution

🧪 🛡️ ⚖️

📖 Definition

A buffer solution resists a change in its pH when a small amount of acid or base is added.

16. Solubility Product

🧂 💧 ⚖️

📖 Definition

The solubility product is the equilibrium constant for the dissolution of a sparingly soluble ionic compound.

Ksp = Product of ionic concentrations
