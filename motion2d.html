1. Scalars and Vectors

🔢 ➡️

📖 Definition

A scalar quantity has only magnitude, whereas a vector quantity has both magnitude and direction.

⭐ Examples:

Scalar: distance, speed, mass, time.
Vector: displacement, velocity, acceleration, force.

2. Representation of a Vector

➡️ 📍

📖 Definition

A vector is represented graphically by a directed line segment. Its length represents magnitude and the arrow shows its direction.

⭐ Important:

Vector notation is commonly written as A⃗ or with an arrow over the symbol.

3. Addition and Subtraction of Vectors

➡️ ➕ ➡️

📖 Concept

Vectors can be added using the triangle law or parallelogram law of vector addition.

⭐ Important:

Vector addition is different from ordinary scalar addition because direction must also be considered.

4. Resolution of a Vector

↗️ ↘️ ↙️

📖 Definition

Resolution of a vector means expressing a vector as two or more component vectors along chosen directions.

Aₓ = A cos θ

Aᵧ = A sin θ
⭐ Important:

Components are especially useful for solving projectile motion and other two-dimensional motion problems.

5. Projectile Motion

🚀 🏹 🎯

📖 Definition

Projectile motion is the motion of an object projected into the air under the action of gravity, neglecting air resistance.

⭐ Important:

Horizontal motion has constant velocity, while vertical motion has constant acceleration due to gravity.

T = 2u sinθ / g

H = u² sin²θ / 2g

R = u² sin2θ / g

⭐ Most Important Derivation

Prove that the trajectory of a projectile is a parabola.

The horizontal displacement is: x = u cosθ × t

The vertical displacement is: y = u sinθ × t − ½gt²

Eliminating t gives an equation of the form: y = Ax − Bx²

Since the equation is quadratic in x, the trajectory of the projectile is a parabola.

6. Motion in a Horizontal Plane

🛩️ ➡️ 🌍

📖 Important Concept

Two-dimensional motion can be studied by resolving the motion into two perpendicular components.

⭐ Important:

The horizontal and vertical motions can be analysed independently.

7. Uniform Circular Motion

🔄 🏎️

📖 Definition

Uniform circular motion is the motion of an object along a circular path with constant speed.

v = rω

a = v²/r = rω²
⭐ Important:

Even though speed is constant, velocity changes continuously because its direction changes.
