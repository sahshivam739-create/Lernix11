🌍 Environmental Chemistry

Chapter 11 • Class 11 Chemistry • Lernix11

1. Introduction

🌍 🧪 🌱

📖 Definition

Environmental chemistry deals with the chemical phenomena occurring in the environment and the effects of pollutants on air, water and soil.

2. Environmental Pollution

🏭 🌫️ 💧

📖 Definition

Environmental pollution is the undesirable change in physical, chemical or biological characteristics of the environment.

⭐ Main Types:

Air pollution • Water pollution • Soil pollution • Noise pollution

3. Air Pollution

🏭 🌫️ 🌍

📖 Concept

Air pollution occurs when harmful substances are present in the atmosphere in concentrations that can affect living organisms and the environment.

4. Major Air Pollutants

🌫️ ⚠️ 🏭
⭐ Important Pollutants:

Carbon monoxide (CO)

Sulphur dioxide (SO₂)

Nitrogen oxides (NOₓ)

Particulate matter

Hydrocarbons

5. Acid Rain

🌧️ ⚠️ 🌳

📖 Definition

Rain or other precipitation made unusually acidic mainly because of atmospheric sulphur and nitrogen oxides is called acid rain.

⭐ Effects:

Damage to plants, aquatic ecosystems, buildings and monuments.

6. Smog

🌫️ ☀️ 🏙️

📖 Definition

Smog is polluted air containing a mixture of smoke, gases and fine particles.

⭐ Types:

Classical smog

Photochemical smog

7. Photochemical Smog

☀️ + 🌫️ = ⚠️

📖 Concept

Photochemical smog forms when nitrogen oxides and hydrocarbons undergo reactions in sunlight.

⭐ Important:

Ozone and other oxidising substances can be formed during photochemical smog.

8. Greenhouse Effect

🌍 ☀️ 🔥

📖 Concept

The greenhouse effect is the warming of Earth's surface and lower atmosphere due to absorption and re-emission of infrared radiation by greenhouse gases.

⭐ Greenhouse Gases:

CO₂ • CH₄ • N₂O • Water vapour

9. Global Warming

🌍 🌡️ 🔥

📖 Definition

Global warming refers to the long-term increase in Earth's average surface temperature.

⭐ Major Cause:

Increase in concentration of greenhouse gases due to human activities.

10. Ozone Layer

🌍 🛡️ ☀️

📖 Importance

The ozone layer absorbs much of the harmful ultraviolet radiation reaching Earth.

11. Ozone Depletion

🛡️ ➖ 🌍

📖 Concept

Ozone depletion means reduction in the concentration of ozone in the stratosphere.

⭐ Important Cause:

Certain chlorine- and bromine-containing compounds can participate in catalytic ozone destruction.

12. Water Pollution

💧 ⚠️ 🐟

📖 Definition

Water pollution occurs when harmful substances enter water bodies and adversely affect their quality and living organisms.

13. Major Water Pollutants

💧 🏭 🧪
⭐ Examples:

Sewage

Industrial waste

Pesticides

Fertilisers

Heavy metal ions

14. Eutrophication

💧 🌿 🐟

📖 Definition

Eutrophication is the enrichment of a water body with nutrients, which can cause excessive growth of algae and depletion of dissolved oxygen.

15. Biological Oxygen Demand

💧 🦠 O₂

📖 Definition

Biochemical oxygen demand (BOD) indicates the amount of dissolved oxygen required by microorganisms to decompose biodegradable organic matter in water.

⭐ Remember:

Higher BOD generally indicates greater organic pollution.

16. Soil Pollution

🌱 🧪 ⚠️

📖 Concept

Soil pollution occurs when harmful chemicals and wastes contaminate soil and affect its quality and organisms.

17. Industrial Waste

🏭 🗑️ ⚠️

📖 Concept

Industrial activities can release gases, liquids and solid wastes that may pollute air, water and soil.

18. Green Chemistry

🌱 🧪 ♻️

📖 Definition

Green chemistry is the design of chemical products and processes that reduce or eliminate the use and generation of hazardous substances.

⭐ Main Goal:

Prevent pollution at the source rather than treating pollution after it has been produced.

⭐ Quick Revision

🧠 🌍 🧪
