⚙️ Laws of Motion

Class 11 Physics • Lernix11

1. Force and Inertia

🧱 💥 ➡️

📖 Definition

Force is an external influence that can change or tend to change the state of motion of an object.

⭐ Inertia:

Inertia is the tendency of an object to resist any change in its state of rest or motion.

2. Newton's First Law of Motion

🚌 🧍 ➡️

📖 Definition

An object remains at rest or continues to move with uniform velocity in a straight line unless acted upon by an external unbalanced force.

⭐ Also called:

The law of inertia.

3. Newton's Second Law of Motion

🏎️ 💨 ➡️

📖 Definition

The rate of change of momentum of an object is directly proportional to the applied force and takes place in the direction of the force.

F = ma

⭐ Important Derivation

Momentum p = mv

According to Newton's second law: F = dp/dt

For constant mass: F = m dv/dt

Since a = dv/dt, therefore F = ma.

4. Newton's Third Law of Motion

🤜 💥 🤛

📖 Definition

For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

⭐ Important:

Action and reaction forces act on two different objects.

5. Momentum and Impulse

⚽ 💥 ⏱️

📖 Momentum

Momentum is the product of mass and velocity.

p = mv

Impulse = Force × Time

6. Free Body Diagram (FBD)

🧱 ⬆️ ⬇️ ➡️

📖 Definition

A free body diagram represents all the external forces acting on an isolated object.

⭐ Common Forces:

Weight, normal reaction, friction, tension and applied force.

7. Block on a Horizontal Surface

🧱 ➡️ 🟫

📖 Concept

The motion of a block on a horizontal surface is analysed by considering all forces acting on the block.

⭐ Important:

Always draw the FBD before applying Newton's laws.

8. Friction

🧱 🧲 ➡️

📖 Definition

Friction is the force that opposes the relative motion or tendency of relative motion between two surfaces in contact.

fₛ ≤ μₛN

fₖ = μₖN
⭐ Types:

Static friction and kinetic friction.

9. Block on an Inclined Plane

🧱 ↗️ 🏔️

📖 Concept

A block on an inclined plane is analysed by resolving forces along and perpendicular to the plane.

Component of weight along plane = mg sinθ

Component perpendicular to plane = mg cosθ

10. Tension and Connected Blocks

🧱 ─── 🧱

📖 Concept

When blocks are connected by a string, their motion is related through the constraint imposed by the string.

⭐ Important:

For an ideal light, inextensible string passing over an ideal pulley, connected blocks have related accelerations.

11. Block and Pulley System

🧱 🪢 ⚙️ 🧱

📖 Concept

Pulley systems are used to analyse the motion of connected bodies by applying Newton's laws to each block separately.

⭐ Problem-Solving Steps:

1. Draw the FBD of every block.
2. Choose positive direction.
3. Apply F = ma to each block.
4. Use the string constraint.

12. Equilibrium of a Particle

⚖️ 🧱 ⚖️

📖 Definition

A particle is in equilibrium when the net external force acting on it is zero.

ΣF = 0

⭐ Quick Revision

🧠 ⚡
Remember:

Newton's Second Law: F = ma

Momentum: p = mv

Static friction: fₛ ≤ μₛN

Kinetic friction: fₖ = μₖN

Equilibrium: ΣF = 0
