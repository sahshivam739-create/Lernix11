🔗 Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure

Class 11 Chemistry • Lernix11

1. Chemical Bond

⚛️ 🔗 ⚛️

📖 Definition

A chemical bond is the attractive force that holds atoms or ions together in a molecule or compound.

⭐ Main Types:

Ionic bond • Covalent bond • Coordinate bond

2. Octet Rule

⚛️ 8️⃣ ⚛️

📖 Statement

Atoms tend to gain, lose or share electrons to achieve a stable configuration similar to the nearest noble gas.

⭐ Exceptions:

Some molecules do not obey the octet rule, such as BF₃ and SF₆.

3. Ionic Bond

➕ 🔗 ➖

📖 Definition

An ionic bond is formed by electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions.

⭐ Example:

NaCl

4. Covalent Bond

⚛️ 🤝 ⚛️

📖 Definition

A covalent bond is formed by sharing of electron pairs between atoms.

⭐ Examples:

H₂ • Cl₂ • CH₄

5. Coordinate Bond

⚛️ ➡️ 🔗 ⚛️

📖 Definition

A coordinate bond is a covalent bond in which both electrons of the shared pair are donated by the same atom.

⭐ Example:

NH₄⁺

6. Lewis Structure

⚛️ •• 🔗 •• ⚛️

📖 Concept

Lewis structures represent valence electrons and chemical bonds using dots and lines around atoms.

⭐ Important:

First count total valence electrons, then arrange atoms and complete their stable electron configurations.

7. Formal Charge

➕ ⚛️ ➖

📖 Formula

Formal charge helps in identifying the most appropriate Lewis structure.

FC = V − L − B/2
Where:

V = valence electrons
L = lone-pair electrons
B = bonding electrons

8. VSEPR Theory

⚛️ ↔️ ↔️ ⚛️

📖 Concept

According to VSEPR theory, electron pairs around the central atom repel each other and arrange themselves to minimise repulsion.

⭐ Repulsion Order:

Lone pair–lone pair > lone pair–bond pair > bond pair–bond pair

9. Hybridisation

🌀 ⚛️ 🌀

📖 Definition

Hybridisation is the intermixing of atomic orbitals of comparable energy to form equivalent hybrid orbitals.

⭐ Important Types:

sp • sp² • sp³ • sp³d • sp³d²

10. Shapes and Hybridisation

📐 ⚛️ 🔺
⭐ Quick Table:

sp → Linear → 180°

sp² → Trigonal planar → 120°

sp³ → Tetrahedral → 109.5°

sp³d → Trigonal bipyramidal → 90°, 120°

sp³d² → Octahedral → 90°

11. Sigma and Pi Bonds

🔗 σ + π

📖 Concept

A sigma bond is formed by head-on overlap of orbitals. A pi bond is formed by sideways overlap of orbitals.

⭐ Examples:

Single bond → 1σ
Double bond → 1σ + 1π
Triple bond → 1σ + 2π

12. Molecular Orbital Theory

⚛️ ➕ ⚛️ ➡️ 🌀

📖 Concept

According to Molecular Orbital Theory, atomic orbitals combine to form molecular orbitals spread over the entire molecule.

Bond Order = 1/2(Nb − Na)

13. Hydrogen Bond

H 🔗 F ••• H 🔗 F

📖 Definition

A hydrogen bond is an attractive interaction involving hydrogen attached to a highly electronegative atom such as F, O or N.

⭐ Types:

Intermolecular hydrogen bonding
Intramolecular hydrogen bonding

⭐ Quick Revision

🧠 🔗 ⚛️
Single bond = 1σ

Double bond = 1σ + 1π

Triple bond = 1σ + 2π

sp = Linear

sp² = Trigonal planar

sp³ = Tetrahedral

Bond Order = 1/2(Nb − Na)
