1. Introduction to Statistics

📖 Concept

Statistics is the branch of mathematics that deals with the collection, organisation, presentation, analysis and interpretation of data.

Data can be represented using tables, graphs and numerical measures.

✏️ Ex 1

Find the number of observations in the data:

4, 7, 9, 12, 15

✅ Solution

There are five values.

Number of observations = 5

✏️ Ex 2

Find the range of:

5, 8, 12, 3, 10

✅ Solution

Range = Highest value − Lowest value

= 12 − 3

= 9

➕ ➗ 📊

2. Mean

The arithmetic mean is obtained by dividing the sum of all observations by the number of observations.

Mean = Σx / n

Where:
Σx = Sum of observations
n = Number of observations

✏️ Ex 1

Find the mean of:

2, 4, 6, 8, 10

✅ Solution

Sum = 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 10

= 30

Number of observations = 5

Mean = 30/5

Mean = 6

✏️ Ex 2

Find the mean of:

5, 7, 8, 10

✅ Solution

Sum = 5 + 7 + 8 + 10 = 30

Mean = 30/4

Mean = 7.5

📊 ✖️ ➗

3. Mean of Grouped Data

For a frequency distribution, each observation is associated with a frequency.

Mean = Σfx / Σf

f = Frequency
x = Observation

✏️ Ex 1

x: 2, 4, 6

f: 1, 2, 1

✅ Solution

Σfx = 2(1) + 4(2) + 6(1)

= 2 + 8 + 6 = 16

Σf = 1 + 2 + 1 = 4

Mean = 16/4

Mean = 4

✏️ Ex 2

x: 5, 10, 15

f: 2, 3, 1

✅ Solution

Σfx = 5(2) + 10(3) + 15(1)

= 10 + 30 + 15 = 55

Σf = 6

Mean = 55/6

📍 📏 📊

4. Median

Median is the middle value of the data when observations are arranged in ascending or descending order.

For odd number of observations:

Median = value of (n+1)/2 th observation

For even number:

Median = average of n/2 th and (n/2 + 1)th observations

✏️ Ex 1

Find the median of:

3, 7, 9, 12, 15

✅ Solution

There are 5 observations.

Median position = (5+1)/2 = 3rd observation.

Median = 9

✏️ Ex 2

Find the median of:

2, 4, 6, 8

✅ Solution

Middle observations are 4 and 6.

Median = (4+6)/2

Median = 5

⭐ 📊 🔢

5. Mode

Mode is the value which occurs most frequently in a data set.

✏️ Ex 1

Find the mode:

2, 3, 3, 4, 5, 3, 6

✅ Solution

The number 3 occurs most frequently.

Mode = 3

✏️ Ex 2

Find the mode:

5, 7, 7, 8, 9, 7, 10

✅ Solution

7 occurs three times.

Mode = 7

6. Measures of Dispersion

Dispersion describes how much the observations are spread around a central value.

Important measures include:

• Range

• Mean Deviation

• Variance

• Standard Deviation

Range = Maximum value − Minimum value

✏️ Ex 1

Find the range of:

10, 15, 18, 22, 30

✅ Solution

Range = 30 − 10

= 20

✏️ Ex 2

Find the range of:

4, 8, 11, 15

✅ Solution

Range = 15 − 4

= 11

σ 📊 🧮

7. Variance

Variance measures the average of the squared deviations from the mean.

Variance = Σ(x − x̄)² / n

where x̄ is the mean.

✏️ Ex 1

Find the variance of:

2, 4, 6

✅ Solution

Mean = (2+4+6)/3 = 4

Squared deviations:

(2−4)² = 4

(4−4)² = 0

(6−4)² = 4

Variance = (4+0+4)/3

Variance = 8/3

✏️ Ex 2

Find the variance of:

1, 2, 3

✅ Solution

Mean = 2

Squared deviations = 1, 0, 1

Variance = 2/3

Variance = 2/3

√ 📊 📐

8. Standard Deviation

Standard deviation is the positive square root of variance.

Standard Deviation = √Variance

✏️ Ex 1

If variance = 25, find the standard deviation.

✅ Solution

Standard deviation = √25

= 5

✏️ Ex 2

If variance = 16, find the standard deviation.

✅ Solution

Standard deviation = √16

= 4
