🔥 Thermodynamics

Class 11 Chemistry • Lernix11

1. Thermodynamic System

🧪 🔥 ⚙️

📖 Definition

A thermodynamic system is a specified portion of the universe selected for studying energy changes.

⭐ Types:

Open system, closed system and isolated system.

2. Surroundings

🧪 🌍

📖 Definition

Everything outside the system that can interact with it is called the surroundings.

3. System and Surroundings

🧪 ↔️ 🌍

📖 Concept

The system and surroundings together constitute the universe. Energy can be exchanged between them depending on the type of system.

4. State of a System

🌡️ ⚖️ 📊

📖 Definition

The state of a system is described by measurable properties such as pressure, volume, temperature and composition.

P, V, T

5. State Functions

📍 ➡️ 📍

📖 Definition

A state function is a property whose value depends only on the initial and final states of the system, not on the path followed.

⭐ Examples:

Internal energy (U), enthalpy (H), pressure (P), volume (V) and temperature (T).

6. Internal Energy

⚛️ ⚡ 🔥

📖 Definition

Internal energy is the total energy present within a system due to the motion and interactions of its particles.

ΔU = U₂ − U₁
⭐ Important:

Internal energy is a state function.

7. Heat and Work

🔥 ➡️ ⚡ ➡️ ⚙️

📖 Concept

Heat and work are two different modes of energy transfer between a system and its surroundings.

⭐ Important:

Heat and work are not state functions.

8. First Law of Thermodynamics

🔥 ➡️ ⚡ ➡️ 🧪

📖 Statement

Energy can neither be created nor destroyed. It can only be converted from one form to another.

ΔU = q + w
⭐ Sign Convention:

q is positive when heat is supplied to the system.

w is positive when work is done on the system.

9. Enthalpy

🔥 📦 ⚡

📖 Definition

Enthalpy is a thermodynamic state function used to describe the heat content of a system at constant pressure.

H = U + PV

ΔH = ΔU + Δ(PV)

10. Enthalpy Change

🔥 ➡️ 🧪 ➡️ ⚡

📖 Concept

The difference between the enthalpy of products and reactants is called enthalpy change.

ΔH = H(products) − H(reactants)
⭐ Exothermic:

ΔH < 0

⭐ Endothermic:

ΔH > 0

11. Heat Capacity

🔥 🌡️ ⚖️

📖 Definition

Heat capacity is the amount of heat required to raise the temperature of a system by one degree Celsius or one kelvin.

C = q / ΔT

12. Hess's Law

🧪 ➡️ 🔥 ➡️ ⚡

📖 Statement

The total enthalpy change of a reaction is the same whether the reaction takes place in one step or several steps.

⭐ Reason:

Enthalpy is a state function.

13. Enthalpy of Formation

⚛️ ➕ ⚛️ ➡️ 🧪

📖 Definition

The enthalpy change when one mole of a compound is formed from its constituent elements in their standard states is called standard enthalpy of formation.

ΔH°f

14. Enthalpy of Combustion

🔥 🧪 ➡️ 💨

📖 Definition

The enthalpy change when one mole of a substance is completely burnt in oxygen under specified conditions is called enthalpy of combustion.

15. Entropy

🧊 ➡️ 💨 🔀

📖 Concept

Entropy is a thermodynamic state function that represents the degree of randomness or disorder of a system.

⭐ Symbol:

S

16. Gibbs Energy

⚡ 🔥 🧪

📖 Important Formula

Gibbs energy helps us determine the spontaneity of a process under suitable conditions.

ΔG = ΔH − TΔS
⭐ Spontaneous Process:

At constant temperature and pressure, a process is spontaneous when ΔG < 0.

⭐ Quick Revision

🧠 🔥 ⚡
ΔU = q + w

H = U + PV

ΔH = H(products) − H(reactants)

C = q / ΔT

ΔG = ΔH − TΔS
