⚛️ Structure of Atom

Class 11 Chemistry • Lernix11

1. Discovery of Electron

⚛️ ➖ 🔬

📖 Concept

Electron was discovered through experiments involving cathode rays. Electrons are negatively charged subatomic particles.

⭐ Charge:

−1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C

2. Discovery of Proton

⚛️ ➕ 🔬

📖 Concept

Proton is a positively charged subatomic particle present in the nucleus of an atom.

⭐ Charge:

+1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C

3. Discovery of Neutron

⚛️ ⚪ 🔬

📖 Concept

Neutron is a neutral subatomic particle present in the nucleus of an atom.

⭐ Charge:

Zero

4. Thomson's Atomic Model

🍪 ⚛️ ➕➖

📖 Model

According to Thomson's model, an atom is a positively charged sphere in which electrons are embedded.

⭐ Limitation:

It could not explain the results of Rutherford's alpha-particle scattering experiment.

5. Rutherford's Atomic Model

🎯 ⚛️ ➡️

📖 Experiment

Rutherford directed alpha particles towards a thin gold foil and observed their scattering.

⭐ Conclusions:

1. Most of the atom is empty space.

2. Positive charge and most of the mass are concentrated in a very small nucleus.

3. Electrons revolve around the nucleus.

6. Limitation of Rutherford Model

⚛️ 🔄 ⚡

📖 Important

Rutherford's model could not explain the stability of the atom and the line spectra of atoms.

7. Bohr's Atomic Model

⚛️ 🌀 🔵

📖 Main Postulates

According to Bohr's model, electrons revolve around the nucleus in certain permitted circular orbits called stationary orbits.

⭐ Important Points:

1. Electrons do not radiate energy while moving in a stationary orbit.

2. Each orbit has a definite energy.

3. Energy is emitted or absorbed when an electron jumps between two energy levels.

mvr = nh/2π

8. Energy of Electron in Hydrogen Atom

⚛️ ⚡ 🔵

📖 Formula

The energy of an electron in the nth orbit of a hydrogen atom is given by:

Eₙ = −13.6/n² eV
⭐ Important:

The negative sign indicates that the electron is bound to the nucleus.

9. Electromagnetic Radiation

📡〰️〰️〰️

📖 Concept

Electromagnetic radiation consists of electric and magnetic fields that oscillate perpendicular to each other and travel through space.

c = νλ
⭐ Where:

c = speed of light
ν = frequency
λ = wavelength

10. Planck's Quantum Theory

⚡ 📦 ⚛️

📖 Main Idea

According to Planck, energy is emitted or absorbed in discrete packets called quanta.

E = hν

11. Photoelectric Effect

💡 ➡️ ⚛️ ➖

📖 Definition

The emission of electrons from a metal surface when suitable frequency electromagnetic radiation falls on it is called the photoelectric effect.

⭐ Important:

The minimum frequency required to eject electrons is called threshold frequency.

12. Hydrogen Spectrum

🌈 ⚛️ 🔵

📖 Concept

The hydrogen atom produces a line spectrum because electrons can occupy only certain quantised energy levels.

1/λ = R(1/n₁² − 1/n₂²)

13. Quantum Numbers

🔢 ⚛️ 🌀

📖 Definition

Quantum numbers are used to describe the state and location of an electron in an atom.

⭐ Four Quantum Numbers:

1. Principal quantum number (n)

2. Azimuthal quantum number (l)

3. Magnetic quantum number (m)

4. Spin quantum number (s)

14. Orbitals

⚛️ ☁️ 🌀

📖 Definition

An orbital is a region of space around the nucleus where the probability of finding an electron is maximum.

⭐ Important:

s orbital → spherical
p orbital → dumbbell shaped

15. Electronic Configuration

⚛️ 🔵 🔵 🔵

📖 Concept

Electronic configuration is the distribution of electrons among different shells, subshells and orbitals of an atom.

⭐ Important Rules:

Aufbau Principle

Pauli Exclusion Principle

Hund's Rule

⭐ Quick Revision

🧠 ⚛️ 🔢
E = hν

c = νλ

mvr = nh/2π

Eₙ = −13.6/n² eV

1/λ = R(1/n₁² − 1/n₂²)
