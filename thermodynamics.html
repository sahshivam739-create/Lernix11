🔥 Thermodynamics

Class 11 Physics • Lernix11

1. Thermodynamic System

🔥 🧪 🌡️

📖 Definition

A thermodynamic system is a specified portion of matter or a region in space selected for studying thermodynamic processes.

⭐ Types:

Open system, closed system and isolated system.

2. Surroundings

🧪 🌍

📖 Definition

Everything outside the thermodynamic system that can interact with the system is called its surroundings.

3. Thermodynamic State

🌡️ 📊 ⚙️

📖 Concept

The state of a thermodynamic system is described by macroscopic variables such as pressure, volume and temperature.

P, V, T

4. Thermal Equilibrium

🌡️ ⚖️ 🌡️

📖 Definition

Two bodies are in thermal equilibrium when there is no net flow of heat between them.

5. Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics

🌡️ = 🌡️ = 🌡️

📖 Statement

If two systems are separately in thermal equilibrium with a third system, they are in thermal equilibrium with each other.

⭐ Importance:

This law provides the basis for the concept and measurement of temperature.

6. Heat

🔥 ➡️ 🌡️

📖 Definition

Heat is energy transferred between a system and its surroundings because of a temperature difference.

⭐ SI Unit:

Joule (J)

7. Work Done in Thermodynamics

💨 🧱 ➡️

📖 Concept

When a gas expands or contracts against an external pressure, work is done by or on the gas.

W = PΔV
⭐ Sign convention:

Work done by the gas is taken as positive in this convention.

8. Internal Energy

⚛️ ⚡ 🔥

📖 Definition

Internal energy is the total microscopic energy associated with the random motion and interactions of particles of a system.

⭐ Important:

For an ideal gas, internal energy depends only on temperature.

9. First Law of Thermodynamics

🔥 ➡️ ⚡ ➡️ 💨

📖 Statement

The heat supplied to a system is used to increase its internal energy and to do work by the system.

Q = ΔU + W
⭐ Important:

The first law is a statement of conservation of energy applied to thermodynamic systems.

10. Isothermal Process

🌡️ = 🔄

📖 Definition

A thermodynamic process carried out at constant temperature is called an isothermal process.

PV = Constant

11. Adiabatic Process

🧪 🚫🔥

📖 Definition

An adiabatic process is a thermodynamic process in which no heat is exchanged between the system and its surroundings.

Q = 0

PVγ = Constant

12. Isobaric Process

📊 ➡️ 🌡️

📖 Definition

A thermodynamic process carried out at constant pressure is called an isobaric process.

P = Constant

13. Isochoric Process

📦 🔒 🌡️

📖 Definition

A thermodynamic process carried out at constant volume is called an isochoric process.

V = Constant

W = 0

14. Second Law of Thermodynamics

🔥 ➡️ ⚙️ ➡️ 🧊

📖 Concept

The second law gives the direction in which natural thermodynamic processes occur and places limitations on the efficiency of heat engines.

⭐ Important:

Heat cannot be completely converted into work in a cyclic process.

15. Heat Engine

🔥 ➡️ ⚙️ ➡️ 💨

📖 Definition

A heat engine is a device that converts part of the heat supplied to it into mechanical work.

Efficiency = W / Q₁

16. Refrigerator

🧊 ❄️ 🔄

📖 Definition

A refrigerator is a device that transfers heat from a colder region to a hotter region by using external work.

COP = Q₂ / W

⭐ Quick Revision

🧠 🔥 ⚡
Remember:

Q = ΔU + W

Isothermal: PV = Constant

Adiabatic: PVγ = Constant

Isochoric: V = Constant, W = 0

Efficiency = W/Q₁

COP = Q₂/W
