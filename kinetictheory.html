1. Kinetic Theory of Gases

⚛️ 💨 ⚛️

📖 Definition

Kinetic theory explains the macroscopic properties of gases on the basis of the motion of their microscopic particles.

⭐ Main Idea:

Gas molecules are in continuous random motion and collide with each other and the walls of the container.

2. Assumptions of Kinetic Theory

⚛️ 🔄 ⚛️
⭐ Important Assumptions:

1. A gas contains a very large number of molecules.

2. Molecules are in continuous random motion.

3. The size of molecules is very small compared with the volume occupied by the gas.

4. Collisions between molecules are perfectly elastic.

5. There are negligible intermolecular forces except during collisions.

3. Pressure of an Ideal Gas

💨 ➡️ 🧱

📖 Concept

Gas pressure is produced because gas molecules continuously collide with the walls of the container.

P = 1/3 ρv²

4. Kinetic Energy of Gas Molecules

⚛️ ⚡ 💨

📖 Concept

The kinetic energy of gas molecules is associated with their random translational motion.

Average K.E. = 3/2 kT

5. Temperature and Kinetic Energy

🌡️ ➡️ ⚛️

📖 Important Relation

The average translational kinetic energy of a molecule is directly proportional to the absolute temperature of the gas.

Average K.E. ∝ T

6. Root Mean Square Speed

⚛️ 💨 📈

📖 Definition

Root mean square speed is the square root of the mean of the squares of the speeds of all gas molecules.

vrms = √(3RT/M)
⭐ Important:

RMS speed increases with the square root of absolute temperature.

7. Degrees of Freedom

⚛️ ↔️ ↕️ 🔄

📖 Definition

The number of independent ways in which a molecule can move or store energy is called its degrees of freedom.

⭐ Examples:

A monatomic gas has 3 translational degrees of freedom. A diatomic molecule has additional rotational degrees of freedom.

8. Law of Equipartition of Energy

⚡ ⚛️ ⚡

📖 Statement

According to the law of equipartition of energy, energy is equally distributed among all independent degrees of freedom at thermal equilibrium.

Energy per degree of freedom = 1/2 kT

9. Specific Heat Capacity of Gases

🔥 ⚛️ 🌡️

📖 Concept

The specific heat capacity of a gas depends on the conditions under which heat is supplied.

⭐ Two Important Values:

CV = Specific heat at constant volume

CP = Specific heat at constant pressure

10. Relation Between CP and CV

🌡️ ⚖️ 🔥

📖 Important Relation

For an ideal gas, the difference between molar specific heats at constant pressure and constant volume is related to the gas constant.

CP - CV = R

11. Mean Free Path

⚛️ ➡️ ⚛️ ➡️ ⚛️

📖 Definition

The mean free path is the average distance travelled by a gas molecule between two successive collisions.

12. Avogadro's Number

⚛️ ⚛️ ⚛️

📖 Definition

Avogadro's number is the number of particles present in one mole of a substance.

NA ≈ 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹

⭐ Quick Revision

🧠 ⚛️ 🔥

P = 1/3 ρv²

Average K.E. = 3/2 kT

vrms = √(3RT/M)

CP - CV = R

NA ≈ 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹
