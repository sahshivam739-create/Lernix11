1. Introduction to Straight Lines

📖 Concept

A straight line is a set of points that extends endlessly in both directions.

The slope of a line tells us how steeply the line rises or falls.

Slope: m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁)

✏️ Ex 1

Find the slope of the line passing through:

(2,3) and (4,7)

✅ Solution

m = (7−3)/(4−2)

= 4/2

m = 2

✏️ Ex 2

Find the slope of the line passing through:

(1,5) and (3,9)

✅ Solution

m = (9−5)/(3−1)

= 4/2

m = 2

📍 📍 ➡️

2. Distance Between Two Points

The distance between two points can be found using the distance formula.

Distance: d = √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²]

✏️ Ex 1

Find the distance between (1,2) and (4,6).

✅ Solution

d = √[(4−1)² + (6−2)²]

= √[3² + 4²]

= √25

= 5

✏️ Ex 2

Find the distance between (2,3) and (6,6).

✅ Solution

d = √[(6−2)² + (6−3)²]

= √[16 + 9]

= 5

📐 ➗ 📏

3. Section Formula

The section formula gives the coordinates of a point which divides the line segment joining two points in a given ratio.

If P divides A(x₁,y₁) and B(x₂,y₂) in ratio m:n,

P = ((mx₂ + nx₁)/(m+n), (my₂ + ny₁)/(m+n))

✏️ Ex 1

Find the point dividing (2,3) and (8,9) in the ratio 1:2.

✅ Solution

x = [1(8) + 2(2)]/3

= 12/3 = 4

y = [1(9) + 2(3)]/3

= 15/3 = 5

Point = (4,5)

✏️ Ex 2

Find the midpoint of (2,4) and (8,10).

✅ Solution

Midpoint = ((2+8)/2, (4+10)/2)

= (5,7)

📈 ✏️ 🔢

4. Equation of a Line

The equation of a straight line represents all the points lying on that line.

Slope-intercept form: y = mx + c

Point-slope form: y−y₁ = m(x−x₁)

✏️ Ex 1

Find the equation of a line with slope 2 passing through (1,3).

✅ Solution

Using point-slope form:

y−3 = 2(x−1)

y−3 = 2x−2

y = 2x + 1

✏️ Ex 2

Find the equation of the line with slope −3 passing through (2,5).

✅ Solution

y−5 = −3(x−2)

y−5 = −3x + 6

y = −3x + 11

📐 🔄 📏

5. Parallel and Perpendicular Lines

Two lines are parallel when their slopes are equal.

Two non-vertical lines are perpendicular when the product of their slopes is −1.

Parallel: m₁ = m₂

Perpendicular: m₁m₂ = −1

✏️ Ex 1

Are the lines with slopes 2 and 2 parallel?

✅ Solution

m₁ = 2

m₂ = 2

Since m₁ = m₂,

The lines are parallel.

✏️ Ex 2 — 🔴 Hard

Are the lines with slopes 3 and −1/3 perpendicular?

✅ Solution

m₁m₂ = 3 × (−1/3)

= −1

Therefore, the lines are perpendicular.

📍 📈 📊

6. Intercepts of a Line

The x-intercept is the point where the line crosses the x-axis.

The y-intercept is the point where the line crosses the y-axis.

Intercept form: x/a + y/b = 1

a = x-intercept
b = y-intercept

✏️ Ex 1

Find the x-intercept and y-intercept of:

2x + 3y = 6

✅ Solution

For x-intercept, put y = 0:

2x = 6

x = 3

For y-intercept, put x = 0:

3y = 6

y = 2

x-intercept = 3, y-intercept = 2

✏️ Ex 2

Find the intercepts of:

4x + 2y = 8

✅ Solution

Put y = 0:

4x = 8

x = 2

Put x = 0:

2y = 8

y = 4

x-intercept = 2, y-intercept = 4

📏 📐 ⭐

7. Angle Between Two Lines

The angle between two lines depends on their slopes.

tan θ = |(m₂−m₁)/(1+m₁m₂)|

✏️ Ex 1

Find the angle between two lines having slopes 1 and 0.

✅ Solution

tan θ = |(0−1)/(1+0)|

= 1

θ = 45°

✏️ Ex 2

Find the angle between lines having slopes 1 and −1.

✅ Solution

m₁m₂ = −1

Therefore the lines are perpendicular.

θ = 90°
