⚛️ Periodicity in Properties

Class 11 Chemistry • Lernix11

1. Modern Periodic Law

🧪 📊 ⚛️

📖 Statement

The physical and chemical properties of elements are periodic functions of their atomic numbers.

⭐ Important:

Modern periodic table is arranged according to increasing atomic number.

2. Modern Periodic Table

📊 ⚛️ 🔢

📖 Structure

The modern periodic table contains 7 periods and 18 groups.

⭐ Blocks:

s-block • p-block • d-block • f-block

3. Atomic Radius

⚛️ 📏 ↔️

📖 Definition

Atomic radius is a measure of the size of an atom.

⭐ Trend:

Atomic radius generally decreases from left to right across a period and increases down a group.

4. Ionic Radius

➕ ⚛️ ➖

📖 Concept

Ionic radius is the effective size of an ion in a crystal or ionic environment.

⭐ Important:

Cations are generally smaller than their parent atoms, while anions are generally larger.

5. Ionization Enthalpy

⚛️ ➡️ ➖ ⚡

📖 Definition

Ionization enthalpy is the minimum energy required to remove the most loosely bound electron from an isolated gaseous atom.

⭐ Trend:

Generally increases across a period and decreases down a group.

6. Electron Gain Enthalpy

⚛️ ➕ ➖ ⚡

📖 Definition

Electron gain enthalpy is the enthalpy change when an electron is added to an isolated gaseous atom.

⭐ Important:

Halogens generally have highly negative electron gain enthalpies.

7. Electronegativity

⚛️ 🧲 ➡️

📖 Definition

Electronegativity is the tendency of an atom in a molecule to attract the shared pair of electrons towards itself.

⭐ Trend:

Generally increases across a period and decreases down a group.

8. Valency

⚛️ 🔗 ⚛️

📖 Concept

Valency is the combining capacity of an element.

9. Metallic and Non-Metallic Character

🔩 ⚛️ 🌿

📖 Trend

Metallic character generally decreases across a period and increases down a group.

⭐ Remember:

Non-metallic character shows approximately the opposite trend.

⭐ Quick Revision

🧠 ⚛️ 📊
Remember:

Atomic radius → decreases across, increases down

Ionization enthalpy → increases across, decreases down

Electronegativity → increases across, decreases down

Metallic character → decreases across, increases down
