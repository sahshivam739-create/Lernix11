⚡ Work, Energy and Power

Class 11 Physics • Lernix11

1. Work Done by a Constant Force

🧱 ➡️ 💪

📖 Definition

Work is said to be done when a force acting on an object produces displacement in the direction of the force.

W = F s cosθ
⭐ Important:

Work is a scalar quantity. Its SI unit is joule (J).

2. Work Done by a Variable Force

📈 💪 🧱

📖 Concept

When force changes with position, the work done is obtained from the area under the force-displacement graph.

⭐ Important:

For a variable force, the F-x graph is very useful for calculating work.

3. Kinetic Energy

🏎️ 💨 ⚡

📖 Definition

Kinetic energy is the energy possessed by an object due to its motion.

K = ½mv²

⭐ Important Derivation

Using Newton's second law and the equation v² − u² = 2as, the work done in accelerating an object from rest leads to:

W = ½mv²

Therefore, kinetic energy K = ½mv².

4. Work-Energy Theorem

💪 ➡️ ⚡

📖 Statement

The net work done on an object is equal to the change in its kinetic energy.

Wnet = ΔK

5. Potential Energy

🏔️ 🧱 ⬆️

📖 Definition

Potential energy is the energy associated with the position or configuration of an object.

Gravitational PE = mgh

6. Conservation of Mechanical Energy

⚡ 🔄 🏔️

📖 Statement

When only conservative forces act, the total mechanical energy of a system remains constant.

K + U = Constant
⭐ Important:

Mechanical energy is the sum of kinetic energy and potential energy.

7. Power

⚙️ ⚡ 🚗

📖 Definition

Power is the rate at which work is done or energy is transferred.

P = W/t

P = Fv
⭐ SI Unit:

watt (W)

8. Conservative and Non-Conservative Forces

🔄 ⚖️ 🧲

📖 Concept

For a conservative force, work done between two points is independent of the path followed.

⭐ Examples:

Gravitational force is conservative, while friction is a non-conservative force.

9. Spring Potential Energy

🌀 🧱 ⚡

📖 Concept

A stretched or compressed spring stores elastic potential energy.

U = ½kx²

⭐ Quick Revision

🧠 ⚡
Remember:

W = Fs cosθ

K = ½mv²

Wnet = ΔK

U = mgh

P = W/t

Spring Energy = ½kx²
