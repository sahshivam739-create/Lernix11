1. Complex Numbers

📖 Concept

A complex number is written in the form:

z = a + ib

where a and b are real numbers and i = √−1.

i² = −1

i³ = −i

i⁴ = 1

✏️ Ex 1

Express √−25 in the form a + ib.

✅ Solution

√−25 = √25 × √−1

= 5i

Answer: 5i

✏️ Ex 2

Simplify:

i²⁵

✅ Solution

Since i⁴ = 1,

25 = 4×6 + 1

Therefore:

i²⁵ = (i⁴)⁶ × i

= 1 × i

= i

2. Algebra of Complex Numbers

Complex numbers can be added, subtracted and multiplied just like algebraic expressions, using i² = −1.

✏️ Ex 1

Add:

(3 + 2i) + (5 − 4i)

✅ Solution

= 3 + 5 + 2i − 4i

= 8 − 2i

Answer: 8 − 2i

✏️ Ex 2

Multiply:

(2 + 3i)(4 − i)

✅ Solution

= 8 − 2i + 12i − 3i²

Since i² = −1:

= 8 + 10i + 3

= 11 + 10i

🪞 + 🅸

3. Conjugate of a Complex Number

If:

z = a + ib

then its conjugate is:

z̄ = a − ib

z × z̄ = a² + b²

✏️ Ex 1

Find the conjugate of:

z = 4 + 7i

✅ Solution

Change the sign of the imaginary part.

z̄ = 4 − 7i

Answer: 4 − 7i

✏️ Ex 2

Find:

(3 + 4i)(3 − 4i)

✅ Solution

= 3² − (4i)²

= 9 − 16i²

= 9 + 16

= 25

📏 🔢

4. Modulus of a Complex Number

If z = a + ib,

|z| = √(a² + b²)

|z|² = a² + b²

✏️ Ex 1

Find the modulus of:

z = 3 + 4i

✅ Solution

|z| = √(3² + 4²)

= √25

= 5

✏️ Ex 2

Find the modulus of:

z = 5 − 12i

✅ Solution

|z| = √(5² + (−12)²)

= √(25 + 144)

= √169

= 13

5. Division of Complex Numbers

To divide complex numbers, multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator.

✏️ Ex 1

Simplify:

(2 + i)/(3 + i)

✅ Solution

Multiply by the conjugate (3 − i):

[(2+i)(3−i)]/[(3+i)(3−i)]

Numerator:

6 − 2i + 3i − i² = 7 + i

Denominator:

9 − i² = 10

= 7/10 + i/10

✏️ Ex 2 — 🔴 Hard

Simplify:

(4 + 3i)/(2 − i)

✅ Solution

Multiply by (2+i):

[(4+3i)(2+i)]/[(2−i)(2+i)]

Numerator:

8 + 4i + 6i + 3i²

= 8 + 10i − 3

= 5 + 10i

Denominator:

4 − i² = 5

= 1 + 2i

6. Quadratic Equations

A quadratic equation is an equation of the form:

ax² + bx + c = 0

where a ≠ 0.

Discriminant: D = b² − 4ac

✏️ Ex 1

Solve:

x² − 5x + 6 = 0

✅ Solution

x² − 5x + 6 = 0

(x−2)(x−3)=0

Therefore:

x−2=0 or x−3=0

x = 2, 3

✏️ Ex 2

Solve:

x² + 4x + 5 = 0

✅ Solution

Here:

a=1, b=4, c=5

D = b²−4ac

= 16−20

= −4

Therefore:

x = [−b ± √D]/2a

= [−4 ± √−4]/2

= [−4 ± 2i]/2

x = −2 ± i

🔍 📊 🧠

7. Nature of Roots

D = b² − 4ac

D > 0 → Two distinct real roots

D = 0 → Equal real roots

D < 0 → Non-real complex roots

✏️ Ex 1

Find the nature of roots:

x² − 4x + 3 = 0

✅ Solution

D = (−4)² − 4(1)(3)

= 16 − 12

= 4 > 0

Therefore, roots are real and distinct.

✏️ Ex 2 — 🔴 Hard

Find k so that the equation

x² + kx + 9 = 0

has equal roots.

✅ Solution

For equal roots:

D = 0

k² − 4(1)(9) = 0

k² − 36 = 0

k² = 36

k = ±6
