⚛️ The p-Block Elements

Class 11 Chemistry • Lernix11

1. Introduction to p-Block Elements

⚛️ 📊 🔵

📖 Definition

The elements in which the last electron enters a p-orbital are called p-block elements.

⭐ Important:

The p-block contains elements of Groups 13 to 18.

2. General Electronic Configuration

⚛️ 🔢 🌀
ns² np¹⁻⁶

The general outer electronic configuration of p-block elements is ns²np¹⁻⁶.

3. Group 13 Elements

🧪 B • Al • Ga

📖 Important Elements

Boron, Aluminium, Gallium, Indium and Thallium belong to Group 13.

General Configuration = ns²np¹

4. Boron

⚛️ B 🔵

📖 Important Properties

Boron is a non-metal and shows several properties different from the other members of Group 13.

⭐ Important Compounds:

B₂H₆ • H₃BO₃ • B₂O₃

5. Aluminium

⚙️ Al ⚛️

📖 Properties

Aluminium is a reactive metal with a protective oxide layer on its surface.

⭐ Important Compounds:

Al₂O₃ • AlCl₃ • Al(OH)₃

6. Group 14 Elements

⚛️ C • Si • Ge

📖 Important Elements

Carbon, Silicon, Germanium, Tin and Lead belong to Group 14.

General Configuration = ns²np²

7. Carbon

💎 C 🪨

📖 Important Properties

Carbon shows catenation and allotropy and forms a very large number of compounds.

⭐ Allotropes:

Diamond • Graphite • Fullerene

8. Silicon

💻 Si ⚛️

📖 Properties

Silicon is a metalloid and is widely used in semiconductor devices.

⭐ Important Compound:

SiO₂

9. Group 15 Elements

⚛️ N • P • As

📖 Important Elements

Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Arsenic, Antimony and Bismuth belong to Group 15.

General Configuration = ns²np³

10. Nitrogen

🌬️ N₂ ⚛️

📖 Properties

Nitrogen is a colourless gas and forms many important compounds. The N≡N bond is very strong.

⭐ Important Compounds:

NH₃ • HNO₃ • NO • NO₂

11. Ammonia

🧪 NH₃ 💨

📖 Formula

Ammonia has the molecular formula NH₃ and has a trigonal pyramidal shape.

⭐ Preparation:

N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃

12. Nitric Acid

🧪 HNO₃ ⚡

📖 Formula

Nitric acid is a strong acid and a powerful oxidising agent in appropriate conditions.

HNO₃

13. Group 16 Elements

⚛️ O • S • Se

📖 Important Elements

Oxygen, Sulphur, Selenium, Tellurium and Polonium belong to Group 16.

General Configuration = ns²np⁴

14. Oxygen

🌬️ O₂ 🔥

📖 Properties

Oxygen is essential for respiration and supports combustion.

⭐ Allotropes:

O₂ and O₃ (ozone)

15. Ozone

🌍 O₃ 🛡️

📖 Importance

Ozone is an allotrope of oxygen and absorbs a significant amount of harmful ultraviolet radiation in the upper atmosphere.

O₃

16. Sulphur

🟡 S ⚛️

📖 Properties

Sulphur is a non-metal that exists in different allotropes.

⭐ Important Compounds:

H₂S • SO₂ • H₂SO₄

17. Group 17 Elements

🧪 F • Cl • Br

📖 Halogens

Fluorine, Chlorine, Bromine, Iodine and Astatine are called halogens.

General Configuration = ns²np⁵

18. Halogens

⚛️ F₂ • Cl₂ • Br₂

📖 Properties

Halogens are highly reactive non-metals and commonly form −1 oxidation state ions.

⭐ Reactivity:

Generally decreases down the group.

19. Hydrogen Halides

H 🔗 Cl 🧪

📖 Examples

Hydrogen halides include HF, HCl, HBr and HI.

⭐ Important:

Their properties change down the group because of differences in bond strength and other factors.

20. Group 18 Elements

🎈 He • Ne • Ar

📖 Noble Gases

Helium, Neon, Argon, Krypton, Xenon and Radon are called noble gases.

ns²np⁶
⭐ Exception:

Helium has electronic configuration 1s².

21. General Trends in p-Block

📊 ⚛️ 🔽
⭐ Important Trends:

Atomic size generally increases down a group.

Ionisation enthalpy generally decreases down a group.

Electronegativity generally decreases down a group.

Metallic character generally increases down a group.

⭐ Quick Revision

🧠 ⚛️ 📚
Group 13 → ns²np¹

Group 14 → ns²np²

Group 15 → ns²np³

Group 16 → ns²np⁴

Group 17 → ns²np⁵

Group 18 → ns²np⁶
