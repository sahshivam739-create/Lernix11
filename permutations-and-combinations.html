1. Fundamental Principle of Counting

📖 Concept

If one operation can be performed in m ways and another operation can be performed in n ways, then both operations together can be performed in m × n ways.

Total ways = m × n

✏️ Ex 1

A shirt can be selected in 3 ways and a pair of trousers in 4 ways. In how many ways can an outfit be selected?

✅ Solution

Number of shirt choices = 3

Number of trouser choices = 4

Total ways = 3 × 4

= 12 ways

✏️ Ex 2

A code consists of one letter followed by one digit. If there are 5 possible letters and 6 possible digits, find the number of codes.

✅ Solution

Letter choices = 5

Digit choices = 6

Total codes = 5 × 6

= 30

n! 🔢

2. Factorial

The factorial of a positive integer n is the product of all positive integers from 1 to n.

n! = n(n−1)(n−2)...3×2×1

0! = 1

✏️ Ex 1

Find 5!.

✅ Solution

5! = 5×4×3×2×1

= 120

✏️ Ex 2

Simplify:

8!/6!

✅ Solution

8!/6!

= (8×7×6!)/6!

= 56

🔄 🔢 🧮

3. Permutations

Permutation means an arrangement of objects in a particular order.

nPᵣ = n!/(n−r)!

✏️ Ex 1

Find 5P2.

✅ Solution

5P2 = 5!/(5−2)!

= 5!/3!

= 5×4

= 20

✏️ Ex 2

In how many ways can 3 students be arranged from a group of 5 students?

✅ Solution

Required ways = 5P3

= 5!/2!

= 5×4×3

= 60 ways

🧩 🔢 👥

4. Combinations

Combination means selecting objects where order does not matter.

nCᵣ = n!/[r!(n−r)!]

nCᵣ = nCₙ₋ᵣ

✏️ Ex 1

Find 5C2.

✅ Solution

5C2 = 5!/[2!3!]

= (5×4)/(2×1)

= 10

✏️ Ex 2

A committee of 3 students is to be selected from 7 students. How many committees are possible?

✅ Solution

Since order does not matter:

Required ways = 7C3

= 7!/(3!4!)

= (7×6×5)/(3×2×1)

= 35 ways

⚖️ 🔄 🧩

5. Difference Between Permutation and Combination

Permutation: Order matters.

Example: ABC and BAC are different arrangements.

Combination: Order does not matter.

Example: Selecting A, B, C is the same selection as C, B, A.

✏️ Ex 1

How many ways can 2 students be selected from 5 students?

✅ Solution

Selection means order does not matter.

Required ways = 5C2

= 10

Answer: 10 ways

✏️ Ex 2 — 🔴 Hard

How many ways can 2 students be chosen and arranged from 5 students?

✅ Solution

Here order matters because students are arranged.

Required ways = 5P2

= 5×4

= 20 ways

🔤 🔄 🧠

6. Permutations of Objects

The number of arrangements of n different objects is n!.

Number of arrangements of n different objects = n!

✏️ Ex 1

In how many ways can the letters of the word CAT be arranged?

✅ Solution

There are 3 different letters.

Number of arrangements = 3!

= 3×2×1

= 6 ways

✏️ Ex 2 — 🔴 Hard

In how many ways can the letters of the word MATH be arranged?

✅ Solution

There are 4 different letters.

Number of arrangements = 4!

= 24 ways

7. Important Properties of Combinations

nC0 = 1

nCn = 1

nC1 = n

nCᵣ = nCₙ₋ᵣ

✏️ Ex 1

Find 8C7.

✅ Solution

Using nCᵣ = nCₙ₋ᵣ:

8C7 = 8C1

= 8

✏️ Ex 2 — 🔴 Hard

Find 10C8.

✅ Solution

10C8 = 10C2

= 10×9/(2×1)

= 45

⭐ Important Formula Revision

1. 0! = 1

2. n! = n(n−1)!

3. nPᵣ = n!/(n−r)!

4. nCᵣ = n!/[r!(n−r)!]

5. nCᵣ = nCₙ₋ᵣ

6. nPᵣ = r! × nCᵣ

7. nC0 = nCn = 1

8. nC1 = n
