1. Centre of Mass

⚫ ⚫ ⚫ 🎯

📖 Definition

The centre of mass is the point where the entire mass of a system may be considered to be concentrated for describing its translational motion.

xcm = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) / (m₁ + m₂)
⭐ Important:

For a uniform symmetrical body, the centre of mass lies at its geometrical centre.

2. Motion of Centre of Mass

🎯 ➡️ 🚀

📖 Concept

The motion of the centre of mass of a system is determined by the net external force acting on the system.

Fext = M acm

3. Linear Momentum of a System

🏎️ 💨

📖 Definition

Linear momentum is the product of mass and velocity.

p = mv
⭐ Conservation:

If the net external force on a system is zero, its total linear momentum remains constant.

4. Torque

🔧 🔄 ⚙️

📖 Definition

Torque is the turning effect of a force about a point or axis.

τ = rF sinθ
⭐ SI Unit:

Newton metre (N m)

5. Angular Momentum

🌀 ⚙️

📖 Definition

Angular momentum is the rotational analogue of linear momentum.

L = r × p

For a rigid body: L = Iω
⭐ Conservation:

When external torque is zero, angular momentum remains constant.

6. Moment of Inertia

⚙️ 🌀 ⚙️

📖 Definition

Moment of inertia is the rotational analogue of mass and measures the resistance of a body to angular acceleration.

I = Σmr²
⭐ Important:

Moment of inertia depends on the mass distribution of the body about the chosen axis.

7. Radius of Gyration

⭕ 📏

📖 Definition

Radius of gyration is the distance from the axis at which the entire mass of a body could be concentrated without changing its moment of inertia.

I = Mk²

8. Parallel Axis Theorem

⚙️ ➡️ 📍

📖 Statement

The moment of inertia about any axis parallel to an axis through the centre of mass is related by:

I = Icm + Md²

9. Perpendicular Axis Theorem

⭕ ⬆️ ⬇️

📖 Statement

For a plane lamina, the moment of inertia about an axis perpendicular to its plane equals the sum of moments of inertia about two mutually perpendicular axes in its plane.

Iz = Ix + Iy

10. Rotational Kinematics

🔄 ⏱️ 🌀

📖 Important Quantities

Angular displacement, angular velocity and angular acceleration describe rotational motion.

ω = ω₀ + αt

θ = ω₀t + ½αt²

ω² = ω₀² + 2αθ

11. Rotational Dynamics

⚙️ 💪 🔄

📖 Concept

Rotational dynamics studies the effect of torque on rotational motion.

τ = Iα

⭐ Important Relation

Torque plays a role in rotational motion similar to the role of force in translational motion.

12. Rolling Motion

🛞 ➡️ 🛣️

📖 Definition

Rolling motion is a combination of translational and rotational motion.

v = Rω
⭐ Important:

In pure rolling without slipping, the point of contact is instantaneously at rest relative to the surface.
