1. Introduction to Hydrocarbons

🧪 C 🔗 H

📖 Definition

Hydrocarbons are organic compounds containing only carbon and hydrogen.

⭐ Main Classes:

Alkanes • Alkenes • Alkynes • Aromatic hydrocarbons

2. Alkanes

C-C-C-C

📖 Definition

Alkanes are saturated hydrocarbons containing only single covalent bonds between carbon atoms.

General Formula = CₙH₂ₙ₊₂
⭐ Example:

Methane → CH₄
Ethane → C₂H₆
Propane → C₃H₈

3. Nomenclature of Alkanes

🔤 🔢 🧪
⭐ Basic Rule:

Select the longest carbon chain and number it from the end nearest to the substituent.

4. Isomerism in Alkanes

C-C-C 🔀 C-C

📖 Concept

Alkanes can show chain isomerism due to different arrangements of the carbon skeleton.

5. Preparation of Alkanes

🧪 ➡️ 🔥 ➡️ C-C
⭐ Important Methods:

Hydrogenation of alkenes

Wurtz reaction

Decarboxylation

🔥 🧪 ⚡
⭐ Important Reactions:

7. Alkenes

C=C 🔗

📖 Definition

Alkenes are unsaturated hydrocarbons containing at least one carbon-carbon double bond.

General Formula = CₙH₂ₙ

8. Preparation of Alkenes

🧪 ➡️ C=C
⭐ Important Methods:

Dehydration of alcohols

Dehydrohalogenation of alkyl halides

9. Electrophilic Addition

C=C ➕ ⚡

📖 Concept

Alkenes commonly undergo electrophilic addition reactions because of their electron-rich double bond.

⭐ Important:

Addition of H₂, X₂, HX and H₂O are common examples.

10. Markovnikov's Rule

C=C ➡️ H-X

📖 Rule

In the addition of an unsymmetrical reagent to an unsymmetrical alkene, the hydrogen generally adds to the carbon already having more hydrogen atoms.

11. Peroxide Effect

⚡ HBr ➡️ C=C

📖 Concept

In the presence of peroxide, addition of HBr to certain alkenes can proceed through a radical mechanism and give the anti-Markovnikov product.

⭐ Remember:

Peroxide effect is mainly observed with HBr.

12. Alkynes

C≡C 🔗

📖 Definition

Alkynes are unsaturated hydrocarbons containing at least one carbon-carbon triple bond.

General Formula = CₙH₂ₙ₋₂

13. Preparation of Alkynes

🧪 ➡️ C≡C
⭐ Important Method:

Double dehydrohalogenation of suitable dihalides can produce alkynes.

14. Reactions of Alkynes

C≡C ➕ ⚡
⭐ Important Reactions:

Hydrogenation

Halogenation

Hydrohalogenation

Hydration

15. Aromatic Hydrocarbons

⭕ ⚛️ ⭕

📖 Definition

Aromatic hydrocarbons contain aromatic ring systems such as benzene.

16. Benzene

⌬ 🧪

📖 Structure

Benzene has the molecular formula C₆H₆ and contains a six-membered ring with delocalised π electrons.

C₆H₆

17. Aromaticity

⭕ 🔄 ⚛️

📖 Concept

Aromaticity is associated with unusual stability arising from cyclic delocalisation of π electrons in suitable planar systems.

18. Electrophilic Substitution in Benzene

⌬ ➕ ⚡
⭐ Important Reactions:

19. Directive Influence

⌬ ➡️ 🔵

📖 Concept

Substituents already present on a benzene ring influence the position at which a new substituent enters.

⭐ Main Positions:

Ortho • Meta • Para

20. Combustion of Hydrocarbons

🔥 C + H ➡️ 💨

📖 Concept

Complete combustion of hydrocarbons generally produces carbon dioxide and water with release of heat.
