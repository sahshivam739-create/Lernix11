1. Wave Motion

A wave is a disturbance that transfers energy from one place to another without the actual transfer of matter as a whole.

✏️ Ex 1

Give one example of a mechanical wave.

Sound waves are mechanical waves.

↕️ 🌊 ↔️

2. Transverse and Longitudinal Waves

In a transverse wave, particles vibrate perpendicular to the direction of wave propagation.

In a longitudinal wave, particles vibrate parallel to the direction of wave propagation.

✏️ Ex 1

Sound wave in air is transverse or longitudinal?

Sound wave in air is longitudinal.

📏 🌊 📐

3. Wavelength

The distance between two consecutive points in the same phase is called wavelength.

Wavelength = λ

v = fλ

✏️ Ex 1

A wave has frequency 5 Hz and wavelength 2 m. Find its speed.

v = fλ

v = 5 × 2

v = 10 m/s

⏱️ 🌊 🔢

4. Frequency, Time Period and Wave Speed

f = 1/T

v = fλ

v = λ/T

✏️ Ex 1

A wave has time period 0.2 s. Find its frequency.

f = 1/T

f = 1/0.2

f = 5 Hz

🔊 🎵 🌊

5. Sound Waves

Sound is a mechanical wave which requires a material medium for propagation.

Sound waves in air are longitudinal waves.

✏️ Ex 1

Can sound travel through vacuum?

No. Sound requires a material medium for propagation.

🎶 🔗 🌊

6. Superposition of Waves

When two or more waves overlap, the resultant displacement is equal to the algebraic sum of their individual displacements.

y = y₁ + y₂ + y₃ + ...

✏️ Ex 1

Two waves have displacements 2 cm and 3 cm in the same direction. Find the resultant displacement.

Resultant displacement = 2 + 3

= 5 cm

🔊 🔊 🎵

7. Standing Waves

A standing wave is formed by the superposition of two waves having the same frequency and amplitude travelling in opposite directions.

Distance between consecutive nodes = λ/2

Distance between node and adjacent antinode = λ/4

✏️ Ex 1

What is the distance between two consecutive nodes?

Distance = λ/2

⭐ 📌 🧠
