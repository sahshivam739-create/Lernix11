1. Periodic Motion

A motion which repeats itself after equal intervals of time is called periodic motion.

Examples: motion of a pendulum and revolution of a planet.

✏️ Ex 1

Give two examples of periodic motion.

✅ Solution

1. Oscillation of a simple pendulum.

2. Revolution of a planet around the Sun.

↔️ 📈

2. Simple Harmonic Motion

Simple harmonic motion is an oscillatory motion in which the restoring force is directly proportional to displacement and is directed towards the mean position.

F = −kx

a = −ω²x

✏️ Ex 1

What is the direction of restoring force in SHM?

✅ Solution

The restoring force is always directed towards the mean position.

⏱️ 🔄

3. Time Period and Frequency

f = 1/T

ω = 2πf

ω = 2π/T

✏️ Ex 1

A particle completes 20 oscillations in 10 seconds. Find its frequency.

f = Number of oscillations / Time

f = 20/10

f = 2 Hz

🪀 ⚙️

4. Spring-Mass System

A mass attached to a spring performs SHM when displaced from its mean position and released.

T = 2π√(m/k)

ω = √(k/m)

✏️ Ex 1

Find the time period when m = 1 kg and k = 4 N/m.

T = 2π√(m/k)

T = 2π√(1/4)

T = 2π × 1/2

T = π s

🔔 🌍 ⏱️

5. Simple Pendulum

A simple pendulum consists of a small bob suspended from a fixed point by a light inextensible string.

T = 2π√(l/g)

For small oscillations

✏️ Ex 1

What happens to the time period if the length of a pendulum increases?

T is proportional to √l.

Therefore, the time period increases.

📈 🔵 ↔️

6. Energy in SHM

Total Energy: E = 1/2 kA²

Kinetic Energy: K = 1/2 k(A² − x²)

Potential Energy: U = 1/2 kx²

✏️ Ex 1

Where is kinetic energy maximum in SHM?

Kinetic energy is maximum at the mean position.
