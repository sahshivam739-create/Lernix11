📏 Units and Measurements

Class 11 Physics • Lernix11

1. Physical Quantities

📏 ⚖️ ⏱️

📖 Definition

A physical quantity is a quantity that can be measured and expressed by a numerical value together with a unit.

⭐ Important:

Examples of physical quantities are length, mass, time, temperature and velocity.

Physical Quantity = Numerical Value × Unit

2. Units

📐

📖 Definition

A unit is a fixed standard quantity used for measuring a physical quantity.

⭐ Important:

A good standard unit should be well defined, easily reproducible and universally accepted.

3. SI System of Units

🌍 📐

📖 Definition

The International System of Units is the internationally accepted system of measurement.

⭐ Seven SI Base Units:

Length — metre (m)
Mass — kilogram (kg)
Time — second (s)
Electric current — ampere (A)
Temperature — kelvin (K)
Amount of substance — mole (mol)
Luminous intensity — candela (cd)

4. Fundamental and Derived Units

🧩 📐

📖 Definition

Fundamental units are independent units, while derived units are obtained by combining fundamental units.

⭐ Examples:

Length → metre (m)
Time → second (s)
Velocity → metre per second (m/s)
Force → newton (N)

5. Measurement of Length

📏 🔬

📖 Important Concept

Length is measured using suitable instruments depending on the size and accuracy required.

⭐ Important Instruments:

Metre scale, Vernier calipers and screw gauge.

6. Accuracy and Precision

🎯 📏

📖 Definition

Accuracy tells how close a measured value is to the true value, while precision tells how close repeated measurements are to one another.

⭐ Important:

A measurement can be precise but not accurate.

7. Errors in Measurement

⚠️ 📐

📖 Definition

The difference between the measured value and the true value is called error in measurement.

⭐ Important Types:

Systematic errors, random errors and least-count related uncertainties are important in measurements.

8. Significant Figures

🔢 ✨

📖 Definition

Significant figures are the digits in a measured quantity that carry meaningful information about its precision.

⭐ Important Rules:

Non-zero digits are significant.
Zeros between non-zero digits are significant.
Leading zeros are generally not significant.

9. Dimensions of Physical Quantities

📦 M L T

📖 Definition

Dimensions of a physical quantity show how it depends on the fundamental quantities such as mass, length and time.

⭐ Important:

The dimensions of velocity are [LT⁻¹].
The dimensions of acceleration are [LT⁻²].
The dimensions of force are [MLT⁻²].

10. Dimensional Formula

🧮 📐

📖 Definition

A dimensional formula represents a physical quantity in terms of powers of fundamental quantities.

Force = [M L T⁻²]

11. Dimensional Analysis

🧠 📊

📖 Definition

Dimensional analysis is a method of using dimensions to study physical relations and equations.

⭐ Uses:

Checking dimensional consistency of equations, finding dimensions of physical quantities and converting units.

12. Limitations of Dimensional Analysis

🚫 📐

📖 Important Concept

Dimensional analysis cannot determine dimensionless numerical constants and cannot establish equations involving addition or subtraction of different terms.
